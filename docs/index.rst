cool-frames
===========

The frame-theory layer of COOL (Computational Orchestration and Listening).

Invertible time-frequency analysis: non-uniform filterbank analysis and
synthesis with auditory, wavelet, constant-Q, Gabor and warped filter design,
frame theory, and phase retrieval — validated against MATLAB LTFAT.
